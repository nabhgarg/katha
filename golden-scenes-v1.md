# Golden Scenes Library v1

**Purpose:** Reference scenes retrieved as few-shot examples at generation time. These demonstrate structure, not final voice.

**Narrator register:** Hindi-dominant Hinglish — Roman script, primary language Hindi, English enters only where an urban Indian person would naturally use it. No literary English constructions.

**Scene structure:**
1. Opening *action* block — establishes room, mood, emotional temperature in Hindi-dominant Hinglish. 3-6 lines.
2. Dialogue flows below the opening block. Characters speak inside the world already established.
3. Brief *action* beats between dialogue lines are allowed — one line max, same Hindi-dominant register.
4. Reader should be able to move from scene context into dialogue without rhythm breaking.

**Format:** Action/setting/internal state in `*asterisks*`. Dialogue outside asterisks. No attribution tags. No quotation marks.

**Tagging:**
- `genre` — ROMANCE | THRILLER | MYTHOLOGY
- `scene_position` — OPENER | MIDPOINT | CLIFFHANGER
- `emotional_beat` — dominant feeling the scene engineers
- `structural_lesson` — one technique this scene demonstrates

### ROMANCE — ANTI-PATTERN

- **genre:** ROMANCE
- **type:** ANTI-PATTERN
- **label:** DO NOT WRITE LIKE THIS

*The wedding venue was noisy and crowded. Rahul was nervous standing in the buffet line. He was attracted to the girl in front of him but didn't know how to talk to her. When the plate slipped, he felt terrible because her kurta was probably expensive.*

Rahul said nervously: "Yaar, I'm so sorry!"

Maya replied in an irritated tone: "It's fine."

Rahul, feeling embarrassed, said: "No yaar this is khaadi na? It's definitely ruined."

*Maya was feeling a mix of emotions. She was annoyed but also somehow interested. The atmosphere was awkward but also romantic. She took the napkins from him and their fingers almost touched, which was a significant moment.*

Maya said: "You don't even know me."

Rahul smiled and said: "Plate twenty-two. You signed before me."

*This surprised Maya. She was impressed by his attention to detail. Outside, the DJ was playing music which added to the romantic atmosphere of the wedding venue.*

Maya said: "You read the seating chart? How weird."

Rahul said shyly: "Main bas bored tha yaar."

Maya introduced herself warmly: "Okay, I'm Maya then."

**Why this fails:** English narrator with literary constructions ("a mix of emotions," "significant moment"). Attribution tags on every line. Generic sensory details ("noisy and crowded"). Random Hinglish inserted for flavour, not at loaded words. Action and character feeling explained, not shown. No concrete sensory detail anchoring the scene.

### THRILLER — ANTI-PATTERN

- **genre:** THRILLER
- **type:** ANTI-PATTERN
- **label:** DO NOT WRITE LIKE THIS

*Priya felt a sense of dread as she stood at her apartment doorway. Something was definitely wrong. The hallway felt cold and threatening. Her hands were trembling with fear and anxiety.*

*Inside, the apartment looked normal but she could feel a strange presence. The atmosphere was tense and suspicious. Everything appeared to be in place but Priya's instincts were screaming.*

*With growing unease, she decided to call the watchman. She was very scared.*

Priya said fearfully: "Hello? Are you there?"

The watchman replied after a suspicious pause: "Ji madam, sab theek hai?"

Priya explained in a panicked voice: "Mera darwaza khula tha! My door was unlocked, I always lock it!"

*There was a very long and uncomfortable pause. Priya's fear increased dramatically.*

The watchman said in a grave tone: "Madam, please immediately bahar nikliye. It is not safe."

*Priya was horrified by this response. She didn't understand what was happening but she knew it was very dangerous. Her heart was beating fast with terror.*

**Why this fails:** English narrator with generic emotional declarations ("dread," "trembling with fear," "terror"). Attribution tags on every line. Adjective-stated emotions ("growing unease," "fear increased dramatically"). No concrete sensory detail — "cold and threatening" tells nothing. Random Hinglish sprinkled for flavour. Character's interior state explained instead of shown through physical action.

### MYTHOLOGY — ANTI-PATTERN

- **genre:** MYTHOLOGY
- **type:** ANTI-PATTERN
- **label:** DO NOT WRITE LIKE THIS

*Kabir stood on the rooftop of his modern Mumbai highrise apartment, smoking a cigarette. The city sprawled below him in all its chaotic glory. He was a regular twenty-six-year-old with a good job and a loving girlfriend. Life was normal.*

*Suddenly, an unusual and supernatural event occurred. The crows arrived in large numbers, which was very strange and mysterious. Kabir felt scared and confused by this cosmic occurrence.*

Kabir said to himself nervously: "Yeh kya ho raha hai yaar? This is so creepy!"

*A mysterious stranger appeared from nowhere. He had an ancient and otherworldly presence that filled Kabir with awe. The atmosphere became mystical and charged with cosmic energy.*

The stranger said in a deep mysterious voice: "Kabir, bahut waqt ho gaya."

Kabir replied in shock and confusion: "Sir, aap galat samajh rahe hain — I'm not Kabir, mera naam Rohan hai!"

*The stranger smiled with ancient wisdom. His eyes contained the knowledge of many lifetimes and Kabir could feel the weight of destiny pressing upon him.*

The stranger explained meaningfully: "Rohan is just your name in this life. Your true naam goes back many janams."

*Kabir felt overwhelmed by this revelation. He could sense his entire worldview changing in this profound cosmic moment.*

**Why this fails:** English narrator with literary declarations ("chaotic glory," "cosmic occurrence," "profound cosmic moment"). Attribution tags on every line. Sensory details are absent — "mysterious" and "otherworldly" do zero work. The supernatural is narrated as cosmic, not physical — the wrongness needs to arrive through the body (crows not cawing, laugh coming out wrong), not through adjectives. Random Hinglish at non-loaded words.
